## Questions that arise during inquiry

- **The familiar word conceals disagreement:** [let the subject determine the question](01-paradigm.md#let-the-subject-determine-what-needs-to-be-understood).
- **The work keeps getting harder inside the same formulation:** [change the formulation](01-paradigm.md#change-the-formulation-when-the-formulation-is-part-of-the-difficulty).
- **More observations do not resolve the uncertainty:** [change the local view](03-structural-lessons.md#change-what-counts-as-a-local-view) and [three different obstacles](07-limits.md#three-different-obstacles-to-understanding).
- **A proposed framework feels elegant but empty:** [generality must return to examples](03-structural-lessons.md#generality-must-return-to-examples-and-comparison).
- **The components work separately but not together:** [compatible recombination](03-structural-lessons.md#local-progress-requires-compatible-recombination) and [intermediate contracts](08-preservation-and-transfer.md#composition-needs-an-intermediate-contract).
- **A counterexample threatens the whole idea:** [error can clarify or defeat](01-paradigm.md#error-can-clarify-a-concept-or-defeat-it).
- **It is unclear whether to keep developing the theory:** [continue, simplify, or stop](05-inquiry-practice.md#continue-simplify-or-stop).
- **A collaborator understands the task but not its point:** [transmit the substance of the question](01-paradigm.md#collaboration-transmits-the-substance-of-a-question).
